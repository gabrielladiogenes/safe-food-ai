
# 07 — Product Outcomes and Evolution

SAFE FOOD AI has been consolidated as a **demonstration MVP of artificial intelligence applied to preventive food communication**, developed to transform consumer requests into contextualized and operationally relevant guidance.

The project evolved from an order interpretation interface into an integrated workflow combining **generative AI, business rules, automation, and asynchronous processing**, prioritizing information fidelity, user experience, and the boundaries of technological responsibility.

## 7.1 Outcomes and Deliverables

Development resulted in the implementation and validation of essential components to demonstrate the technical and functional feasibility of the proposed solution.

| Deliverable | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Web application | Functional MVP developed with Next.js, React, and TypeScript |
| Artificial intelligence | Google Gemini integration for contextual recommendations |
| Business rules | Interpretation of dietary declarations and order modifications |
| Automation | Demonstration workflow integrated with Make and WhatsApp notifications |
| Asynchronous processing | Request-tracking structure using Redis |
| Software quality | Manual testing, automated testing, and functional validation |
| Technical documentation | Bilingual documentation of the product's architecture, evolution, and validation |

The main deliverable was demonstrating how **generative models can be integrated into business processes with explicit rules, exception handling, and human supervision**, without relying exclusively on AI-generated interpretations.

## 7.2 Applied Technical Competencies

The project provided practical experience in competencies related to developing artificial intelligence solutions for business:

- **Advanced prompt engineering:** structuring, refining, and controlling generative model instructions.
- **AI and API integration:** communication between the application, external services, and language models.
- **Process automation:** building demonstration workflows with Make and notification integrations.
- **Product development:** translating real-world needs into functional requirements, business rules, and user experience.
- **Quality and validation:** automated testing, qualitative evaluation of generative responses, and failure handling.
- **Architecture and reliability:** asynchronous processing, data validation, and separation between deterministic and generative logic.

Development also required **continuous learning, investigation of technical problems, and decision-making guided by test results**, connecting knowledge of technology, analytics, and business.

## 7.3 Next Steps and Product Evolution

SAFE FOOD AI remains a demonstration MVP, with opportunities to evolve into a product with greater technical and operational maturity.

Planned next steps include:

- **Quantitative AI evaluation:** establishing metrics for fidelity, consistency, and relevance of generated recommendations.
- **Expanded test coverage:** incorporating new scenarios, edge cases, and regression checks.
- **Observability:** implementing structured monitoring of latency, failures, and integration behavior.
- **Security and scalability:** improving access controls, data protection, and processing capacity.
- **User validation:** evaluating usability and the suitability of recommendations in supervised operational scenarios.

Evolution toward a commercial environment would depend on additional validation, privacy and security requirements, authorized integrations with establishments, and specialized assessment of food-related risks.

SAFE FOOD AI demonstrates a development approach focused on **solving real-world problems through the combination of artificial intelligence, automation, and business logic**, emphasizing functional, responsible, and continuously evolving solutions.
